# Every New Research Has A Lightweight Confirmation

Before each new Deep Research Run begins, the TUI presents a system-proposed approach
and asks the Primary User to accept or adjust it. This deliberate confirmation creates
a useful habit of reviewing research intent before cost and evidence gathering begin.
It must remain a concise, plain-language research choice rather than a technical setup
screen, an internal lifecycle interruption, or a troubleshooting burden.

One universal confirmation boundary also avoids a separate heuristic that tries to
guess whether a question is clear enough to bypass user review. The workflow is
therefore stable: question, proposal, confirmation or adjustment, then research.
