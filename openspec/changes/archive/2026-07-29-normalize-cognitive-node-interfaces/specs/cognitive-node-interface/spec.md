> req: CNI-001, CNI-002, CNI-003, CNI-004, CNI-005

## Purpose

Define a complete, non-runtime maintenance-reader interface for every logical Deep
Research node, while preserving the code, typed contracts, specs, and tests that own
behavior as the sole execution authorities.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Future cognitive reader projections use the product-responsibility-first fixed interface

Each current logical node SHALL colocate exactly one non-runtime `workflow.md` reader
projection. It SHALL lead with a specific Product responsibility, then expose these
separate fields in this exact semantic order: Participation mode, Commitment state,
source-audited Current operating mechanism, Primary cognitive/control program
surface, Deterministic authority boundary, and audit-only Current model-branch
evidence. The title SHALL be `Node — Product Responsibility`; the card SHALL use, in
order, Node Identity, From Symptoms, Three Cross-Module Facts, Route Facts, and
Evaluation and Verification Order. The fixed card SHALL not create or rewrite a
runtime prompt, tool permission, model branch, human interaction, or graph behavior.

#### Scenario: Reader opens an active cognitive program
- **WHEN** a maintainer opens a projection for a current direct-model-loop node
- **THEN** the card identifies its product responsibility and bounded cognitive
  participation before routing the maintainer to model-visible policy/context/tool/
  feedback seams and the separate deterministic admission and route owners

#### Scenario: Reader opens a non-active current mechanism
- **WHEN** a maintainer opens bootstrap, rerun, HITL2, readiness, or final delivery
- **THEN** the same card shape distinguishes controller exclusion, conditional human
  decision, or deferred cognition from the source-audited current mechanism without
  fabricating a prompt, tool, candidate, user choice, or executable route

### Requirement: Product identity and current model-branch evidence remain separate and closed

The reader inventory SHALL contain one individual card for every logical node in the
source-owned topology denominator and SHALL not reduce the inventory to current
direct model branches. Its validation SHALL reject a missing node, grouped node
identity, missing commitment state, or a Charter/direct-branch label used as product
responsibility, participation mode, commitment state, primary program surface, or
deterministic authority boundary. Current branch evidence remains audit-only; it does
not assert a permanent product role or model behavior.

#### Scenario: Inventory covers active and deferred nodes
- **WHEN** the reader inventory is checked against the logical topology
- **THEN** the check establishes exact eleven-node set equality, including all six
  active and five non-active current mechanisms, without accepting a group label or
  direct-branch count as proof

#### Scenario: Branch evidence is not an identity field
- **WHEN** a card tries to describe itself as `node-agent` or `no-agent`
- **THEN** validation rejects that identity and directs the reader to its product
  responsibility and separately labelled, audit-only current branch evidence

### Requirement: Reader projections preserve deterministic authority seams

Every card SHALL distinguish a model candidate or human choice from the deterministic
writer that admits it or writes state, an artifact, checkpoint, lifecycle result, or
route. Its symptom navigation SHALL name the existing parser, validator,
materializer, gate, ledger, publisher, node handler, or typed contract holding that
authority. The card SHALL not assign candidate acceptance, publication, state
mutation, checkpoint mutation, or executable routing to Markdown or a model.

#### Scenario: Candidate reaches deterministic admission
- **WHEN** a reader investigates an active cognitive candidate
- **THEN** the card routes it to the current deterministic admission owner and does
  not attribute artifact publication, state mutation, or route choice to the model

#### Scenario: Controller receives trusted input
- **WHEN** a reader investigates bootstrap or rerun
- **THEN** the card explains the trusted binding or rerun-scope seam and its
  deterministic transition without inferring a model or human-decision layer

### Requirement: Deferred and conditional participation require separately admitted behavior

A deferred cognitive or conditional human-decision card SHALL name its current
fallback/controller and the later bounded behavior change required for activation.
Deferred cognition SHALL identify the future question, authorized model-visible input
boundary, candidate schema, feedback/evaluation need, and deterministic admission
owner as prerequisites, without choosing their behavior. A conditional human-decision
card SHALL state the genuine non-inferable preference or authorization that remains.
An intentional controller exclusion SHALL state why model or human participation is
the wrong seam.

#### Scenario: Future activation is considered
- **WHEN** a maintainer considers a readiness critic, report composer, or HITL2
  interaction
- **THEN** the card directs them to a separately admitted behavior change rather than
  adding an inferred model loop, interrupt, tool posture, or route

### Requirement: Reader inventory is statically validated without runtime participation

The project SHALL provide a deterministic, zero-external-dependency checker and
focused contract evidence for the eleven reader projections. It SHALL validate only
topology coverage, fixed card identity/heading structure, required separation of
fields, and the non-runtime authority notice. It SHALL not load, render, parse for
runtime, score cognitive quality, determine lifecycle state, validate arbitrary
source/test references, or certify that an existing model branch is correct.

#### Scenario: Reader shape drift is detected
- **WHEN** a required card, identity/heading field, or authority notice is missing,
  or a current branch is conflated with product identity
- **THEN** the focused deterministic check fails with the logical node and violated
  reader constraint while no runtime behavior is executed
