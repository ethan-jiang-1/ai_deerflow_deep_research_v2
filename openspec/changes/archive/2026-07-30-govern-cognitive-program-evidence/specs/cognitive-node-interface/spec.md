## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Deferred and conditional participation stays separately admitted

Deferred cognitive and conditional human-decision cards SHALL name the current
fallback or controller and the separately admitted activation behavior required later.
Fulfilled activation cards SHALL instead state their current accepted participation,
source-audited active mechanism, direct model-branch evidence, and retained
deterministic authority. Neither state SHALL infer a prompt, interrupt, tool posture,
candidate, or route that current source and its owning specification do not provide.

The project SHALL retain exactly one unresolved non-runtime activation dossier for
HITL2. It SHALL state HITL2's product responsibility, human-decision/authorization
participation mode, `conditional/unresolved` commitment state, autonomous current
mechanism, non-inferable preference or irreversible authorization precondition,
trusted and untrusted input boundary, typed human-choice boundary, deterministic
admission owner, bounded failure posture, lowest deterministic proof seam, and an
explicit fresh-change admission boundary. That boundary SHALL require a newly proposed
and separately reviewed behavior change after the product precondition is approved; it
SHALL not name the archived no-change disposition as an active successor or claim a
current interrupt, prompt, model branch, route menu, or approved user-decision
experience.

Readiness and final delivery SHALL no longer appear in the unresolved activation
dossier denominator after their separately approved activations. Their reader cards
SHALL record `current accepted` bounded cognitive participation and their
source-audited active zero-tool critic/composer mechanisms while retaining the hard
rules, materializer, renderer, gate, publisher, and graph owners that admit candidates
or write routes and lifecycle facts. No dossier or reader card SHALL create runtime
behavior, implementation authority, or a future Scope Card approval.

#### Scenario: Conditional HITL2 activation is considered
- **WHEN** a maintainer considers a future HITL2 decision experience
- **THEN** the sole unresolved dossier requires a genuine user-approved decision
  trigger, typed visible outcome, and fresh separately reviewed behavior change rather
  than inferring a raw internal-route menu or reusing an archived no-change change

#### Scenario: Fulfilled activation projections report current facts
- **WHEN** a maintainer reads the readiness or final-delivery card after activation
- **THEN** it reports the current accepted active model loop and its deterministic
  admission/route authority instead of the fulfilled deferred fallback

#### Scenario: Dossier cannot invent an active program
- **WHEN** the unresolved HITL2 dossier is validated
- **THEN** it contains no inferred interrupt, model call, prompt, tool posture, route,
  or claimed live judgment result

#### Scenario: Projection cannot grant authority
- **WHEN** a reader card or activation dossier changes
- **THEN** production behavior remains owned by approved specifications, typed
  contracts, source, and executable tests rather than the projection
