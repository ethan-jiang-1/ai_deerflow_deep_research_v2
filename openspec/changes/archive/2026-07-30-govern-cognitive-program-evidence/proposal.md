## Why

Deep Research now has eleven logical nodes and twenty direct model branches, but its
review evidence remains split across node, branch, workflow, claim, and requirement
registries, while an older requirement and two activation projections still describe
the pre-activation six-node/eighteen-branch state. Maintainers need one compact,
mechanically joined board that reports current responsibility and proof honestly and
makes any later test consolidation preserve named risks instead of chasing a count.

## What Changes

- Add one test-owned cognitive-program evidence board with exactly eleven individual
  logical-node rows and twenty individual direct-branch rows, joined to the topology,
  prompt catalog, capability cohort, workflow coverage, node conformance, central claim
  catalog, calibration registries, and requirement-impact owners rather than becoming
  another runtime authority.
- Keep product responsibility, participation mode, commitment state, source-audited
  current mechanism, deterministic authority, and audit-only model-branch evidence as
  separate node facts. Record bounded node hypotheses and collected node proof, and
  give every direct branch exact calibration-case/central-claim links covering all
  forty-one current calibration cases, live-evaluation applicability, and a known
  limitation without using a Charter binary or branch count as node identity. A
  collected live selector proves provenance and applicability, not a successful live
  run or model quality.
- Permit `human-decision` only for node-board proof of HITL2's conditional admission
  boundary. Keep active-branch closing links classified as `cognitive-program`,
  `deterministic-guardrail`, or `wiring`, with `obsolete-duplicate` non-closing; reject
  missing or grouped rows, false active-loop claims, under-specified programs, unknown
  or uncollected evidence, and proof assigned to the wrong owner.
- Reconcile the cognitive-program requirement text to the canonical twenty-branch
  denominator and reconcile the non-runtime reader/dossier projections after readiness
  and final-delivery activation. HITL2 remains a distinct conditional/unresolved human
  decision; fulfilled readiness/final-delivery activation dossiers no longer claim
  current deferred mechanisms, and the HITL2 dossier requires a fresh separately
  reviewed behavior change rather than naming the archived no-change change as an
  active successor.
- Add explicit consolidation-decision governance: a proposed retirement must map every
  displaced requirement/risk to collected retained proof that preserves that exact
  named risk. Any seam, evidence-class, or authenticity difference requires bounded
  rationale; this change approves no retirements, deletes no tests, and defines no
  target test count or universal evidence-strength ordering.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `cognitive-program-evidence`: reconcile the active-branch contract to twenty exact
  rows and require the complete eleven-node/twenty-branch governance board and joins.
- `evaluation-hardening`: govern evidence consolidation through named preserved risks,
  proof-class context, collected replacement proof, and an explicit
  no-count/no-implicit-deletion boundary.
- `cognitive-node-interface`: make reader and activation-dossier projections reflect
  fulfilled readiness/final-delivery activations while retaining HITL2's unresolved
  human-decision contract and the non-authority boundary.

## Change Focus

- **Primary module / causal owner:** `agent/tests/assets/`, where test-owned node,
  branch, central-claim, and requirement-impact projections are joined and validated.
- **Question:** How can reviewers establish exact current cognitive-program coverage
  and preserve the risks proved by selected tests without turning review metadata,
  aggregate counts, or Charter labels into behavior authority?
- **Necessary adjacent/external contracts:**
  `graph/topology.py::LOGICAL_NODES` answers the exact node denominator;
  the prompt catalog and `COHORT_EVIDENCE` answer the exact direct-branch denominator
  and capability reference; `NODE_CONFORMANCE` and `MODEL_WORKFLOW_COVERAGE` answer
  collected node/loop proof; the central claim catalog and `REQUIREMENT_IMPACTS` answer
  selector collection and preserved-risk ownership; the four calibration registries
  answer exact branch-to-evaluation applicability; cognitive-node reader/dossier
  contracts answer current non-runtime participation projections and the fresh-change
  admission boundary for any future HITL2 interaction.
- **Evidence seam:** focused invalid-fixture tests around the evidence-board and
  consolidation validators, exact set-equality joins against the current topology/catalog,
  exact branch/calibration-case/central-claim joins, reader/dossier contract tests, and
  the integrated test-asset checker.
- **Not in scope:** node prompts or capability Markdown; tool posture; model, provider,
  candidate admission, graph, route, checkpoint, artifact, or runtime behavior; test
  deletion or marker relabeling; live or release execution; backend or frontend changes.
- **Triggered charter policies:** authority-and-projections, change-admission

## Impact

The change is contained to Deep Research test-owned evidence assets and validators,
their focused contract/graph tests, the non-runtime readiness/final-delivery reader and
activation-dossier projections, requirement registry/impact metadata, and the three
listed OpenSpec specifications. It introduces no public API, dependency, production
import, runtime state, graph topology, backend, or frontend change.
