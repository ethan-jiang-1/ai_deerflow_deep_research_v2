> req: WSN-006

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Wave2 calibration preserves accepted-evidence synthesis boundaries

The Wave2 synthesis and its existing zero-tool repair SHALL make model-visible the bounded
criteria for evidence-grounded findings, relations, uncertainty, and honest gaps. The initial
request SHALL receive only graph-assigned topic context, accepted submission references and
evidence, and the trusted output contract. A
repair SHALL receive only the same bounded accepted-evidence assignment, its invalid draft as
untrusted data, and a compact closed category derived from its parser or semantic validation result;
it SHALL not receive a raw exception, retrieve, add evidence or references,
or receive artifact, checkpoint, gate, route, or retry authority. A candidate gap SHALL remain
distinct from the deterministic searchable-gap projection. The existing parser, semantic
validator, materializer, preview builder, and Wave2 gate SHALL remain the only owners that admit
content, persist an artifact, derive a searchable-gap projection, or select an outcome.

#### Scenario: Calibrated synthesis keeps unsupported claims as honest gaps
- **WHEN** a valid Wave2 candidate is generated from graph-assigned accepted evidence but a
  proposed finding or relation lacks that assignment's support
- **THEN** the candidate policy requires it to omit the unsupported content or record a bounded
  honest gap, while deterministic validation and materialization remain the only admission path

#### Scenario: Repair cannot convert feedback into new evidence or routing authority
- **WHEN** the existing Wave2 repair receives an invalid initial draft
- **THEN** it uses only the bounded draft, compact parser/semantic-failure category, and same
  accepted-evidence assignment with no tools, and an invalid repair publishes neither a synthesis artifact nor a
  searchable-gap projection or route
