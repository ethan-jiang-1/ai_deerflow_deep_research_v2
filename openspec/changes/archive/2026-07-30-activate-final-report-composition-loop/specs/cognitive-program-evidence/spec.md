## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Final report composition closes the twentieth direct-branch row

The active cognitive-program ledger SHALL contain exactly twenty individual direct
model-branch rows, including `final-delivery/composer`. Its row SHALL identify the
readiness-bounded layout-order input projection, final capability/catalog/bridge
evidence, deterministic renderer/parser/publisher/gate ownership, absence of model
feedback authority, and a separately classified composition-evaluation disposition.
(`CPE-003`)

#### Scenario: Final composer evidence is not hidden by owner aggregation
- **WHEN** maintainers inspect the active branch ledger
- **THEN** final-delivery composition has one independent row rather than inheriting
  evidence from readiness or another publication branch
