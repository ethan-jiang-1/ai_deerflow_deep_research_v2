# Workflow Control Profile

> authority: guidance only; exact state, decisions, recovery, and outcomes remain in owning contracts
> trigger: changing state/control placement, human decisions, recovery, terminal handling, participant outcomes, or workflow outcome review

Enable this profile for state/control placement, human decisions, recovery, terminal
handling, or participant outcomes. Select every triggered rule below.

## Control And Recovery

> trigger: adding retry, fallback, cancellation, terminal handling, or a control check

Name the direct fact, recovery owner, closed eligible condition, attempt/time bound,
cancellation behavior, exhaustion disposition, and nearest legal action. Reject
unbounded retry, silent fallback, guessed resume, and presentation-owned recovery.

## Human Interaction Integrity

> trigger: adding or revising a human decision, semantic input, visible control, or interaction recovery

Identify the semantic subject, visible material facts, input interpreter, deterministic
effect owner, legal controls, stale/ambiguous path, recovery bound, and transcript
proof. Human input is a typed decision candidate, not a prompt convention or route.

## Participant Outcomes

> trigger: changing a person- or machine-consumed lifecycle outcome

Human and machine projections derive from the same typed facts. People receive a
comprehensible outcome and legal next action; machines receive stable bounded fields.
Neither projection controls lifecycle state. Preserve the owning disclosure boundary.

## Control Placement

> trigger: moving a candidate, judgment, direct fact, admission, or recovery boundary

Use one review table with: Changed decision or fact; Cognitive candidate or human
judgment; Direct fact and deterministic owner/evaluator; Design posture; Protected
invariant or legal recovery; Reuse or complexity removed/avoided; Deterministic
evidence seam. Closed postures are `advisory`, `bounded-repair`, `human-decision`, and
`non-bypassable`. A human-decision row also selects human-interaction integrity.

## Workflow Outcome Review

> trigger: changing a model, tool, provider, worker, retry, terminal, diagnostic, or lifecycle projection path

Record each failure class, fact owner, recovery owner and bound, terminal disposition,
legal next action, and deterministic evidence seam. The table exposes missing
decisions but cannot authorize recovery or replace lifecycle authority.
